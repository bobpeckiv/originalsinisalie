More words worth knowing

  • Sin

    Greek hamartia, an archer's word for missing the mark. Not a stain, a miss. You aim again.

  • Original sin

    Not in Genesis, and not in the Gospels. It took its shape with Augustine around 400 CE, in his fight with a British monk named Pelagius, who thought we were born good.

  • Christ

    Not Jesus' last name. Greek Christos, "anointed." Mystics use "Christ consciousness" for the state he attained, which is open to everyone.

  • Gnostic

    From gnosis, knowing by experience. The early Christians who said the kingdom is found by knowing yourself, until the orthodox church buried their books. Some turned up in an Egyptian jar in 1945.

  • Avatar

    Sanskrit for one who "descends": the One taking human form. Krishna, the Buddha, and Jesus are often named together. Masks of the same face.
