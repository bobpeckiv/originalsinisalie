Verses / John 8:58

The verse

"Before Abraham was, I AM."

John 8:58

Abraham lived something like two thousand years before Jesus. He's the father of the Jewish people. So when a Jewish teacher says "before Abraham was, I AM," it can't be a literal statement about a man's birthday. It's a statement about something that doesn't live in time at all.

The verse

The line that almost got him stoned

Jesus said to them, "Most certainly, I tell you, before Abraham came into existence, I AM." Therefore they took up stones to throw at him.

John 8:58–59

His listeners heard it immediately. "I AM" is the name God gives Moses at the burning bush: "I AM THAT I AM" (Exodus 3:14). Jesus wasn't claiming to be older than Abraham. He was speaking as the eternal awareness that was never born and never dies.

The I AM sayings

John is full of them

John's Jesus says "I am" again and again: the bread of life (6:35), the light of the world (8:12), the door (10:9), the good shepherd (10:11), the resurrection and the life (11:25), the way, the truth, and the life (14:6), the true vine (15:1). Read them as the I AM speaking, not a man bragging, and they open up. (More on John 14:6.)

Jesus himself makes the distinction. "Why do you call me good? No one is good but God alone" (Mark 10:18, NRSV). "By myself I can do nothing" (John 5:30, NIV). The man and the I AM aren't the same thing. The man let the I AM shine through completely.

Every tradition

"I am" is the doorway

  • "Be still, and know that I am God." Psalm 46:10
  • Sri Ramana Maharshi taught one practice above all: ask "Who am I?" and rest in the pure sense of I AM before any thought attaches to it.
  • Nisargadatta Maharaj's most famous book is called I Am That.
  • The Sufi Mansur al-Hallaj was executed in 922 for saying Ana al-Haqq, "I am the Truth."

Before any of us was born, I AM. That's not Jesus' private property. It's what he was trying to show us we are.
