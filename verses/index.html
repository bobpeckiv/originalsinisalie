Short takes

More verses worth knowing

  • "The kingdom of God is within you."

    Luke 17:21. The Greek entos points inward. Some translations say "among you," but either way it's here and now, not a reward for later.

  • "Before Abraham was, I AM."

    John 8:58. A Jewish teacher claiming to predate the father of the Jewish people. It only makes sense if the I AM is eternal awareness, not a man's resume.

  • "Why do you call me good? No one is good but God alone."

    Mark 10:18. Jesus himself drawing a line between the man and the divine.

  • "To you has been given the secret of the kingdom."

    Mark 4:10–11. Jesus explaining that crowds get parables and the inner circle gets the mystical teaching.

  • "If you bring forth what is within you, what you bring forth will save you."

    Gospel of Thomas, saying 70. The verse that didn't make it into the Bible, and maybe the whole path in one line.
